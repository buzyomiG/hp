Take photos of your face from various angles to find the "Best Angle".

### 【Main Features】

・Take selfies from various angles

・Voice guidance when taking photos

・View photos in the Gallery screen

・Take photos at the Best Angle

・View photos in the Best Gallery screen

・Save photos from the Best Gallery screen to your device (up to 6 photos in the free version)

・Send photos to other apps, email, or a printer via the share menu in the "Best Gallery"
